1. **Corporate law / corporate governance** - business formation, corporate structuring, governance, cross-border transactions, and business advisory.
2. **M&A** - business mergers and acquisitions, including cross-border transactions.

- **Patent legal services are not to be included** in the requested scope.  This is important because ALPS stated that it is not currently writing patent firms.
- The materials contain website references to export controls, sanctions, trade compliance, intellectual property, and other adjacent services.  Unless the firm instructs otherwise, do **not** allocate application percentages to those areas or describe them as current insured services for this submission.
